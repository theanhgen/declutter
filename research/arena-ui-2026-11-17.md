## What this changes against today (15d82ad)

- Popup: iOS green switches → ON / OFF words; segmented control → ink pill; blue "report" link → plain text.
- Settings: stats as dot + label + count rows. Remove the pitch from the tip line ("declutter is free. if it saves you
  clicks, leave a tip."): keep a plain "tip jar" link.
- Container apps: the tip section stays, no persuasion copy.
- Site: hero shows the before/after page; a bundled Martian Mono replaces `ui-monospace`.
- Icon and wordmark: sketches only; a brand decision for the owner.
